Featured Case

Mobile Cinema — Reaching Remote Communities Through Film

Challenge

UNICEF needed to reach remote communities across Timor-Leste with critical health and education messages — in areas without TV or internet access.

Production

Created animated characters, produced short films, and toured 12 districts with mobile cinema over 8 weeks with a crew of 15.

Result

40,000+ direct viewers. Extended to radio and social media reaching 200,000+ people. Selected for international development film showcase.

Frequently Asked Questions

What production services does Pixelasia offer in Timor-Leste?

Pixelasia offers full-service film and documentary production, commercials and social behaviour change campaigns, event and festival management (including DIFF), radio and audio production through M3 Radio Dili, and comprehensive production services for international crews filming in Timor-Leste.

Can Pixelasia support international film crews in Dili?

Yes. Pixelasia is the only full-service production house in Timor-Leste and has been providing local production support since 2011. Our multicultural crew from six countries bridges international production standards with deep local knowledge, handling permits, locations, equipment, local crew, and logistics throughout Timor-Leste.

What is the Dili International Film Festival?

The Dili International Film Festival (DIFF), organized by Pixelasia since 2019, brings screenings, masterclasses, and cross-cultural exchange to Timor-Leste. It connects local filmmakers with international voices and is building Southeast Asia's newest film community.
