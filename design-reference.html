Social Behaviour Change Campaign — Mobile Cinema

Challenge
UNICEF needed to reach remote communities across Timor-Leste's districts with critical health and education messages — areas where TV and internet don't reach.
Production
Pixelasia developed animated characters "Super Trainer" and "Feto Fantastiku", produced short films, and toured 12 districts with a mobile cinema setup over 8 weeks.
Result
40,000+ direct viewers in remote communities. Campaign extended to radio and social media, reaching 200,000+ people. Selected for international development film showcase.
